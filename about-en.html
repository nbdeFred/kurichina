Heritage

The Origin of Our Name

A name steeped in millennia of wisdom — a founding commitment to recognizing and elevating talent

"Kuri" draws its name from Cao Zhi's Ode to Emperor Ku, an ancient tribute that traces the Emperor's lineage back to the Yellow Emperor through Xuan Xiao, celebrating a sovereign who governed with virtue, brought peace to heaven and earth, and whose wisdom shone as brightly as the sun and moon.

Emperor Ku valued integrity above all, and possessed an uncanny ability to recognize and elevate talent. When Yi demonstrated archery skills unmatched in the realm, Emperor Ku appointed him as Master Archer, bestowing upon him the crimson bow and sacred arrows. To seek out and elevate those of exceptional ability — this is the essence of headhunting. Kuri China takes its name from this ancient wisdom, carrying forward the mandate of recognizing talent and matching it with purpose.
